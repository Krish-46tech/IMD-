SCORING METHODS

How the detectors work

01 / Continuity

Token-set Jaccard similarity between adjacent steps. Abrupt vocabulary shifts raise the cue score.

02 / Language cues

Simple negation and contrast markers are counted. They can signal revision, but are not proof of a mistake.

03 / Numeric shift

Numbers are extracted and compared with earlier values as a weak consistency cue. Arithmetic is not verified.

04 / Thresholding

The earliest step score crossing the selected threshold is returned.

05 / Learned model

A TF-IDF logistic model trained on ProcessBench prefixes; threshold selected on held-out validation problems.

Research boundaryThe classifier score is not a calibrated probability or proof of correctness. ProcessBench covers math reasoning only, and neither detector establishes that an error cannot be recovered.
